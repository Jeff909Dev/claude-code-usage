Spend on this MacAPI-equivalent cost from your local transcripts: today, 7 and 30 days, models and projects.

install

  1. Download ClaudeUsage.zip and unzip it.
  2. Move Claude Usage.app to /Applications.
  3. First launch (it isn't notarized): open the app once, then click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Or, before opening it, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Claude Usage.app"

requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • Claude Code installed and signed in at least once (claude on your PATH)
